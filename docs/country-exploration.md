# Country exploration

Selecting a destination with Take me somewhere, tapping a country on the globe, or opening a country from Atlas does not mark it explored. One continuous clay progress line stays below the country toolbar and fills smoothly with the percentage of the card scrolled. The existing paper, pine, serif type and glass controls remain the visual language.

Progress is the furthest scroll offset divided by the available scroll distance, from zero at the top to 100 percent at the bottom. It updates during every scroll without section milestones or a reading pause. Scrolling back, reopening the card or loading more content never reduces saved progress. The bar follows fractional progress continuously; persistent writes are coalesced at each whole percentage and when scrolling stops or the card closes. Music links remain optional and do not require a subscription or external playback.

At the end, hold the leaf for 900 milliseconds, or pull up past the bottom and keep holding. A clay ring fills, changes to a pine check, and confirms that the country is saved to the journal. Releasing early cancels. VoiceOver and other assistive activation use the same completion action without a timed gesture. Reduce Motion removes the progress, ring and scale animations.

Scroll progress is a reading cue, not proof of comprehension. A fast scroll can reach 100 percent, but confirmation is still required to mark a country explored. Progress survives closing and relaunching on this device and stays within the existing guest/account workspaces. It is included in local data exports and erased with local data. Partial progress is not uploaded or shared with friends. Only confirmed completion uses the existing exploration sync and sharing preference. No backend change is required.

Previously explored countries remain explored. Reopening or refreshing them does not change their exploration dates. Bookmarks, private notes, feed retries and source attribution retain their existing behavior. Opening a country from Atlas dismisses search keyboard focus, so it does not obscure the journal controls when the sheet closes.

## Checking a country reading

The country card opens with one or two sentences that locate the country for a first-time reader. The next card tells one short story about a historic place, object or tradition. Both readings ship with the app. Each has its own source link. The historical fact is the same on every device.

When reviewing a new reading or a correction, use these steps.

1. Open the country-specific source and match the place, community and time period to the claim. UNESCO’s descriptions of inscribed sites and practices, museum object records, national archives and published research are good starting points. A country homepage or search result cannot establish a detail about a particular object. [World Heritage List](https://data.unesco.org/explore/dataset/whc001/) and [Intangible Heritage List](https://data.unesco.org/explore/dataset/ich001/).
2. Write the title and body in your own words. Include a detail a reader can picture. Keep the source’s dates, uncertainty and local scope. For disputed origins or a claim of being first, check an independent specialist source and narrow the wording to what both accounts support.
3. Store the overview, publisher and direct link in the country’s `introduction` in `countries.json`. Update the corresponding ISO entry in `historical-facts.json`. In the review description, identify the supporting passage and explain decisions about conflicting accounts. An approved correction ships in the next app build.
4. Run `bash apps/ios/Scripts/test_readings.sh`. It reads the packaged content through the same Swift models used by the app and checks all 195 country IDs, source links, overview lengths, and historical fact IDs and bodies. The reviewer checks the actual meaning of each changed claim against the source.

**November 2026 review.** The initial 195 facts were checked against the linked descriptions or records. For UNESCO material, the source record’s country code was checked before its description was used, including shared sites such as the stone circles in Senegal and The Gambia. Morocco’s card describes the traditional attribution of the Fez mosque to Fatima al-Fihri in the ninth century. Historical accounts differ about the founder, and treating the mosque as a modern university in its founding year would add a disputed claim. [IRCICA architectural inventory](https://www.islamicarchitecturalheritage.com/listings/mosque-and-university-karaouiyn), [Aga Khan Trust for Culture](https://the.akdn/en/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/akaa/rehabilitation-al-qaraouiyine-mosque) and [discussion of the medieval evidence](https://brill.com/display/book/edcoll/9789004427952/BP000004.pdf).

Geographic overviews use country-specific maps or physical geography from the archived 2020 World Factbook. For administrative changes, consult current official sources. The catalog includes the 2026 name Naoero alongside Nauru, the current capital of Equatorial Guinea, and Sri Lanka’s administrative capital. [World Factbook archive](https://simonw.github.io/cia-world-factbook-2020/), [UN country-name record](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/893164), [Equatorial Guinea’s January decree](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/decreto_ley_por_el_que_se_declara_la_ciudad_de_la_paz_djibloho_capital_de_la_republica_de_guinea_ecuatorial) and [Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mfa.gov.lk/national-profile-and-geography).

## Validation

`CountryReadingUITests` exercises Take me somewhere without premature exploration, shared progress between Discover and Atlas, gradual scroll progress, backward scrolling without regression, partial progress across relaunch, cancelling a short hold, completing and reopening, bottom pull and hold, unavailable feeds and accessibility text sizes. The globe selection test separately checks that previewing countries leaves their discovery incomplete and does not increase explored history. These tests use simulator-only content fixtures without provider or paid AI calls. Journal checks cover scroll geometry, continuous fractions, monotonic persistence, legacy decoding, sync isolation, confirmation, duplicate completion and workspace reset/restore.

Simulator results and the review video are kept under ignored `outputs/country-reading-progress/`. The owner's authorized TestFlight candidate is **1.0.1 (13)**; package and availability evidence are recorded in [TestFlight status](testflight.md) and ignored `outputs/testflight/1.0.1-13/`. App Store and backend releases remain separate.
