Who is responsible

WorldNiche is an independent app operated by Reda Boutayeb in Los Angeles, California, United States. This policy covers the WorldNiche iPhone app and worldniche.app. For privacy requests, contact boutayeb.reda@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

You can discover countries and keep a journal without an account. The app stores saved countries, explored countries, reading history used to avoid repeat discoveries, and preferences on your device. Creating an account is optional. You choose a display name, which can be a nickname, and can add a photo or use your initials.

Ask Niche and supported AI cultural observations use Apple's on-device models. WorldNiche does not send your questions or companion conversations to a remote AI service. Availability depends on your device and Apple Intelligence settings. Conversations are not saved as a server-side chat history.

Sign-in sessions are stored in iOS Keychain. Exported files may contain personal data; any copy you save or share is under your control. iOS device backups may contain local app data according to your Apple backup settings.

Photos and permissions

Adding a profile photo is optional. Apple's system photo picker gives WorldNiche only the photo you select, without access to your whole library. The app removes original camera and location metadata before uploading the selected image to private storage.

During profile setup, you can allow WorldNiche to send your submitted name and photo to OpenAI for safety checks before sharing them with friends. Your choice is remembered for later edits, so you do not need to grant permission for each upload. If you decline, a person reviews changes instead. You can change your choice in Settings. Withdrawing permission stops future automatic checks but cannot recall a request already sent.

You can see your saved changes immediately. Friends continue to see your previous approved profile while an update is checked. Authorized moderators may access submissions to resolve concerns. You can replace or remove your photo in Profile → Edit profile; without a photo, your initials are used. WorldNiche does not request your physical location or import your address book. Sharing an invitation uses Apple's share sheet.

What friends can see

Friends can see your chosen display name and photo, or initials if you skip the photo. They cannot see your sign-in email. New profiles start with exploration sharing on, with a visible choice during profile setup. Existing accounts keep their previous preference. While sharing is on, current friends can see countries you explore in the app after signing in. Your guest exploration history is not automatically uploaded. Turning it off removes your shared exploration records from the active service. Saved-only journal entries are not shared.

Tapping a valid invitation connects its sender and signed-in recipient automatically. If the recipient is signed out, sign-in happens first. Anyone holding a valid link may be able to use it, so share it intentionally. The signed-in app receives the sender's profile information while processing the invitation. You can revoke invitation links without removing existing friends, remove a friend, or block an account. Invitation preview pages do not expose your profile or redeem the link.

Removing a connection limits future service access. It cannot erase screenshots or other copies someone has independently saved.

Public content and external links

Music, news, cultural reading, and images are requested directly from Apple, Google, Wikimedia, and the content hosts shown in the app. Those requests can reveal your IP address and the requested country, article, or image to the provider. Following a source or song link opens that provider's service.

See the policies from Apple, Google, and Wikimedia. WorldNiche's app and website do not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, advertising cookies, or cross-site tracking pixels. Hosting and service logs are different from analytics and may still record technical requests.

Retention and deletion

Account, profile, and social information stays in the active service while your account exists, unless you remove it through an available control. Invitation status, profile submissions, moderation decisions, and abuse reports may remain for security and abuse handling until related account deletion or service cleanup. Open reports are kept for review and are not automatically removed just because they are old. We do not treat signing out or uninstalling as a request to delete an online account.

Short-lived security records include request timestamps linked to an account, without copies of submitted names or photos. Account deletion removes that link. These records are scheduled for daily removal once they are more than 25 hours old and may remain longer if cleanup is delayed.

Delete account and all data removes your stored profile photo, authentication account, profile, friendships, shared explorations, invitations, blocks, reports, profile submissions, and moderation decisions linked to that account from the active service. The app clears its local account data after the service confirms deletion. Apple-linked deletion includes authorization revocation, with an explicit manual-removal recovery option if needed. If deletion cannot complete, the app shows an error or pending state so you can retry or contact support.

Provider security logs, email delivery records, and backup copies are separate from active app records. They may remain under the providers' retention and recovery controls after active account deletion. A local clear does not erase those records or copies you saved elsewhere. Contact us for questions about a particular request or retained record.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up, and meet applicable legal obligations. Avoid including information we do not need.

Your choices and privacy requests

In Profile → Settings, you can change exploration sharing and your profile safety preference, revoke invitation links, export your data, clear this device, or delete your account. See the deletion steps and what each option does.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing. You can turn exploration sharing off during profile setup or later in Settings without losing access to discovery. For other requests, email boutayeb.reda@icloud.com. We may need to verify account ownership before releasing or changing private data. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children and policy updates

WorldNiche is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact boutayeb.reda@icloud.com so the account can be reviewed and the information removed where appropriate. Local age requirements may differ.

We update this page when practices change and show the revision date at the top. Changes requiring a new permission or choice will be presented before that feature uses the information.
