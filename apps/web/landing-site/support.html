This is the contact for WorldNiche support and abuse reports. You do not need a WorldNiche account to get in touch.

Help us understand what happened

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, WorldNiche version, and the steps that led to the problem. For content issues, include the country and source title. A screenshot can help, but crop out private details.

Never send your password, six-digit sign-in code, Apple verification code, session credentials, or a private friend invitation link. We will not ask for those.

A few quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Discover, Atlas, and your local journal work without one. An account is needed to connect with friends.

My email code has not arrived.

Check the address and spam folder. Use the app's resend option when it becomes available, and enter the newest code. If delivery still fails, contact us without including the code.

A friend link opened the website.

With WorldNiche installed, tap Open in WorldNiche on the invitation page. If the invitation has expired or been revoked, ask your friend for a new link. Sign in when prompted. A link from your own account cannot add you as your own friend.

Music, news, or a cultural source is missing.

Availability differs by country and source. Refresh the affected card, check your connection, or follow its source link. A missing chart does not mean that a country has no popular music.

Why is Ask Niche unavailable?

It requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence enabled and the on-device model ready. Discovery and source links remain available without it.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account and all data. Clearing this device is a separate action. Read the full deletion guide.

Report a profile or a security issue

Open a friend's profile to remove, block, or report them. Blocking is separate from reporting. If you cannot use the app, send the profile name, the issue, and only the details needed to investigate to boutayeb.reda@icloud.com.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, use the private security contact instead of a public issue. See our security page and community guidelines.
