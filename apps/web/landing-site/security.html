The safeguards around your account, profile, and connections.

Last updated October 27, 2026

PrivacySecuritySupportCommunityDelete your account

Account access

WorldNiche uses Sign in with Apple. Existing email accounts can recover access with a one-time code. There is no WorldNiche password to remember. Session credentials are stored in iOS Keychain, and the app connects to its account service over HTTPS.

Service-side authorization checks determine which account data each signed-in user can read or change. Account administration credentials are kept out of the app and public source code.

Control your sharing

New profiles start with exploration sharing on, with a visible choice during profile setup. Existing accounts keep their saved choice. Only accepted friends can read shared country records. Turning sharing off removes those records from the active service. Profile photos use private storage with access limited to the owner and current friends. The app removes original camera and location metadata before preparing a selected photo for upload.

Invitation links expire and can be revoked. Blocked accounts cannot reconnect through an invitation. Viewing the website or a message preview does not create a friendship.

You can revoke unused invitations, remove friends, or block someone in the app. Only share invite links with people you intend to connect with.

A quiet website

This website uses HTTPS and does not embed advertising, analytics trackers, or third-party video players. The demo is hosted here. Invitation pages use a policy that prevents sending the invitation address as a referrer to another site.

Technical infrastructure can still produce access and security logs. The privacy policy explains the services involved and the limits of deletion.

Found something that needs attention?

Email boutayeb.reda@icloud.com with a description, the affected feature, and safe steps to reproduce it. Use a test account you control. Do not access another person's data, disrupt the service, or include live credentials and invite links.

Please report privately so the issue can be investigated. This page describes implemented safeguards, not a certification or a promise that any system is invulnerable.

Safer profiles and connections

We check profile names and photos before sharing them with friends. Your saved changes appear immediately to you. If an update needs more time, friends continue to see your previous approved profile.

You choose whether to allow automatic safety checks during profile setup, and can change your preference in Settings. The privacy policy explains how submitted content is handled. Reports stay private. You can remove or block a connection and report content that does not belong here.
