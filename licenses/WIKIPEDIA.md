# Wikipedia text attribution

Wikipedia text shown by WorldNiche is attributed to **Wikipedia contributors**. Each live reading includes its article title and a link to the source article; the article’s history identifies its contributors. Retrieval time and the source revision timestamp are retained when available.

Wikipedia article text is generally available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See [Wikipedia’s reuse guidance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights#Re-use_of_text) and the article’s own notices for applicable terms.

Live extracts are limited to 110 words, normalize whitespace, and show an ellipsis if shortened. This is an excerpting/formatting change, not an endorsement by Wikipedia or its contributors. Retain the attribution and license information when redistributing Wikipedia text or adaptations; the license’s ShareAlike terms apply to adapted material.

Source links on editorial fallback notes also identify Wikipedia material used as evidence. Independently expressed facts do not imply ownership of the underlying articles. Photographs, logos, recordings, and other media can have separate licenses; this text notice does not license them.
