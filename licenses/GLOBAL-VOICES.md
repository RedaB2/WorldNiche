# Global Voices reporting

WorldNiche displays original headlines, bylines, dates and links from Global Voices' English country feeds. The text is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), subject to any item-specific exception. See the publisher's [republishing guidelines](https://globalvoices.org/about/global-voices-attribution-policy/) and [feed directory](https://globalvoices.org/feeds/), checked October 29, 2026.

The app credits Global Voices and the author beside each headline, links to the original story, and includes a license link in both country details and onboarding. It does not republish article bodies, photographs, audio or video. Headlines are presented as supplied, with XML decoding and whitespace normalization. No endorsement is implied.

The bundled directory contains 193 matching country feeds. Andorra and Micronesia are not listed by the publisher. Empty feeds, unavailable feeds and stories older than a year do not become invented headlines. Dates remain visible. Coverage is editorial and varies by country; it is not a country's top-news ranking.

The feed parser rejects entries without a byline, usable date or original HTTPS Global Voices link. It replaces XML-forbidden control characters before parsing because the live South Korea feed contained one in an unused article body. It still rejects malformed XML. Music and news remain independently cached and refreshed.

The replacement ships starting with build 7. Earlier beta builds used Google News RSS and must not be selected for the production submission.
